## Overview
This knowledge base is maintained by an LLM and serves as a comprehensive repository for concepts, entities, comparisons, and summaries related to AI agents, RAG, and advanced system design. It organizes complex information into structured learning paths and technical comparisons.

## Key Concepts
*   **Agent & Intelligence:** Focuses on Agent frameworks, workflows (e.g., RAG), and skill operationalization (SkillsOps).
*   **RAG Strategies:** Covers core retrieval methods, incremental updates, advanced techniques like mixed retrieval, re-ranking, and chain-of-RAG.
*   **Framework Comparisons:** Provides comparisons between different Agent frameworks (LangChain vs LangGraph vs DeepAgents) and knowledge retrieval methods (RAG vs KAG).
*   **Entities:** Introduces specific tools and projects, such as Obsidian CLI and TradingAgents.
*   **Learning Summaries:** Offers complete guides and summaries for learning AI agents and foundational AI concepts.

## Key Insights
*   The knowledge base emphasizes the evolution of Agent frameworks (LangChain to LangGraph) and the necessity of advanced RAG techniques for production environments.
*   There is a structured approach to learning by providing comprehensive summaries (e.g., Agentic AI Guide, Hello-Agents) alongside specific technical concepts.
*   Practical application is addressed through entity introductions (tools like Obsidian CLI, TradingAgents) that bridge theory and implementation.
*   The structure facilitates understanding the trade-offs between different architectural choices, such as vector retrieval versus knowledge graphs.
